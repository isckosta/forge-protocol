# Code of Conduct

Forge is an engineering community. Professional disagreement is expected. Personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

Participants should criticize ideas rather than people, provide technical reasoning when possible, acknowledge uncertainty, accept contradictory evidence, avoid harassment and discriminatory conduct, respect contributor time, and keep discussions relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not tolerate harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexual harassment, sustained personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Technical disagreement alone is not misconduct.
