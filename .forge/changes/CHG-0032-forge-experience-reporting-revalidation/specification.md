# Specification — CHG-0032 Forge Experience Reporting Revalidation

## Revalidation scope

- FER is disabled by default and has no effect on normal Forge validation.
- Explicit enablement permits local, Git-native report creation.
- Reports preserve structured observations, uncertain classification, positive
  evidence, safe provenance, and non-normative follow-up candidates.
- Privacy, malformed input, symlink, concurrency, and write-failure behavior
  remains isolated from Change state.

No Protocol, Contract, Flow, Gate, Change lifecycle, telemetry, or analytics
change is included.
