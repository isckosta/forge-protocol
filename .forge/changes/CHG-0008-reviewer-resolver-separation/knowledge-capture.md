# Knowledge Capture — CHG-0008

Durable knowledge is captured in ADR-0008, canonical C-026, Protocol Specification §25, Review Policy, schemas, tests, and this Change record.

The key limitation is permanent unless superseded by a future decision: isolated sessions of the same underlying model reduce shared-context contamination but do not remove correlated model bias. `agent_different_model` remains future work.

A second durable constraint is evidence truthfulness: pending Strict Review must not invent reviewer identity merely to satisfy structural validation. Identity becomes mandatory when actual FULL Review execution begins.
